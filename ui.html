How the geometry works

Insertion direction is +Z. At each XY position we find the model's lowest surface, then clear everything above it to the rim. Upper silhouettes extend vertically so the physical object can slide straight down.

This version uses a conservative sampled heightfield, not exact CAD subtraction. Small details are softened and cavity edges can be wider by several grid cells. Choose a finer pitch for better fit. The floor remains at least the specified thickness. Closed, clean models work best; open or damaged scans may produce unexpected cavities.

The output is a single closed triangle mesh. STEP is tessellated on import. STL export is in mm; no STEP export. Gridfinity mating feet and a separate matching baseplate are available. Check your slicer and test-fit before a final print.
